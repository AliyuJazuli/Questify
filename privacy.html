Privacy

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0. This policy explains what Questify stores on your device and how its optional browser notifications work.

Data stored on your device

Questify is a static app with no account or Questify user-data backend. Your profile name, habits, challenges, check-ins, XP, badges, saved Sticky Notes, preferences (including pinned-widget positions), and notification history are saved in this browser's local storage. A Lock In note stays with its active session unless you save it to Sticky Notes. The app's offline files are cached by its service worker.

This data stays in the browser storage on your device; it is not synchronized to Questify servers. Clearing this browser's site data removes the saved progress and offline cache. If you use multiple browsers or devices, your data does not automatically sync between them.

Browser notifications

Daily reminders are optional. Questify asks for browser notification permission only after you turn on Daily Reminders in Settings. When permission is granted, Questify may show up to one reminder per day when it checks for overdue or due-today challenges.

You can switch reminders off in Questify Settings or revoke the site's permission in your browser or device settings. Quiet focus suppresses new Questify toasts, notification-log entries, and browser reminders during a Lock In session; it cannot recall notifications already delivered. It does not silence other apps or system alerts. Browsers do not provide web apps permission to change system Do Not Disturb/Focus settings.

Sticky Notes windows

A saved note opened in its own window continues to use the same local storage as Questify. Edits and pin status sync between open Questify windows in the same browser profile.

Hosting and external resources

The app files are delivered by its hosting provider. Like other websites, the host may process basic connection information needed to serve those files. Questify does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Questify loads its display font from Google Fonts when online. Your browser may contact Google's font service to retrieve it; the app remains usable with system fonts if that service is unavailable. The app itself does not send your habits or progress to Google Fonts.

Your choices

  • Turn daily reminders on or off in Settings and manage notification permission in your browser.
  • Use Reset All Data in Settings to erase Questify progress stored by the app.
  • Clear Questify site data in your browser to remove local storage and offline files.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if Questify's data practices change. The policy on this page describes the current app behavior.
